## 🛠️ How to Add the Content to BlueCrest

### Method A: Fast UI Entry via Sanity Studio (Recommended)
1. Open the local CMS dashboard: **`http://localhost:3000/studio`** (or your live studio domain).
2. Click **"📰 Articles / Longform Posts"** (or **"⚡ Daily News Desk"**).
3. Click the top-right **"Create new document"** (`+`) button.
4. Fill in the 3 tabs generated by the AI:
   - **Tab 1: Content**:
     - Paste `Title`, click **Generate** for `Slug`.
     - Paste `Excerpt`.
     - Upload your generated image (or download from Midjourney/DALL-E) and paste the `Alt Text`.
     - Paste the `Body` text into the Portable Text editor.
   - **Tab 2: Editorial & Attribution**:
     - Select `Category`, `Author`, and `Tags`.
     - Toggle `Featured` or `Trending` if you want it on the homepage hero.
   - **Tab 3: SEO & Social Sharing**:
     - Paste `SEO Meta Title` and `SEO Meta Description`.
5. Click the green **"Publish"** button at the bottom-right (Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + P` on Mac, `Ctrl + Alt + P` on Windows).
6. **Done!** The article is instantly live and synced on `/blog` or `/news` with full JSON-LD structured data.
